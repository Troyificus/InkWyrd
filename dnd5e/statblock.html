Custom Variables

For "to hit" bonuses, use [STRHIT] / [DEXHIT] / [INTHIT] / [WISHIT] / [CHAHIT] (pick whichever ability the attack uses — Str/Dex for weapons, Int/Wis/Cha for spells depending on caster type) — not the bare [STR] token. [STR] alone only inserts the raw ability modifier (e.g. +3); [STRHIT] adds your Proficiency Bonus for you and gives the actual number that goes after "+X to hit" (e.g. +6). [STRSAVE] etc. give the matching save DC (e.g. 15). Also available: [AC], [HP], [CR], [PB], plus any custom variable you define below, referenced the same way with [TOKEN].
