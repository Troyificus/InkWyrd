What it does

  • Music — playlists that crossfade on their own, with per-track volume and fades.
  • Soundboard — effects and looping ambience (rain, taverns, wind) on buttons you arrange yourself.
  • Scenes — one press changes the playlist and ambience for the whole mood.
  • Your voice — your mic through noise suppression and your own VST3 plugins, with optional music ducking.
  • Discord built in — no virtual audio cable or DAW. It also works without Discord, through your own speakers.

Windows may warn about the download the first time you run it, because the app isn't code-signed yet. The GitHub page explains what to expect, and the source is published so you can see exactly what it does.

InkWyrd Audio is a separate project, hosted on GitHub. Inspired by Kenku FM and Winamp.
